# MAS v0.4 Control Testing — Design and Operating Effectiveness

The 30 MAS controls now have explicit Test of Design (ToD) and Test of Operating Effectiveness (ToE) procedures in the workbook and normalized Control Testing Brain.

## Authority boundary

These procedures are **human-authored draft testing procedures**. They are not presented as verbatim regulatory text. `requirements/mas.yaml` remains the requirement authority for the controls that have formal requirement definitions. The testing procedures describe how the organization can test design and operation against those requirements/expectations.

## Test model

- **ToD:** establishes whether the control is suitably designed, approved, attributable, scoped and testable.
- **ToE:** establishes whether the control operated over a defined population/sample and whether exceptions were addressed.
- **PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTABLE:** are determined from evidence; lack of evidence is not automatically proof that the control does not exist.
- Sampling remains risk-based; where the population is small or critical, full-population testing may be appropriate.

## MAS sources

The workbook's Primary MAS source column identifies the source basis for each control. For controls referencing current MAS technology-risk obligations, use the currently applicable MAS notice/licence conditions at the time of testing rather than relying on a stale copy. MAS' Technology Risk Management Notice is published by MAS and its scope varies by regulated entity/licence.
