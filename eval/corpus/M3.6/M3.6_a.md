# Model Validation Summary — CRS-2026-04 Collections Prioritisation Model

Independent Model Validation | Group Risk
Ref: IMV/2026/041
Circulated: 14 August 2026

**Distribution:** Head of Collections Analytics, Model Owner (D. Rajaratnam), Model Risk
Committee (for noting at the September meeting), Internal Audit (read-only)

---

## 1. Scope of this review

CRS-2026-04 replaces the CRS-2024-11 scorecard used to sequence outbound collections
contact for unsecured retail exposures. The model was built by Collections Analytics.
This review was performed by Independent Model Validation, which sits under Group Risk
and reports to the CRO. No member of the review team contributed to model development,
feature selection or training. Validation was performed on the candidate build dated
2026-07-02, on a holdout the development team did not have access to during training.

Materiality tier: 2 (moderate). Reviewed under the standard validation scope rather than
the enhanced scope reserved for tier 1.

## 2. What we tested

| Test | Method | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Discrimination | AUC on out-of-time holdout (Jan–Jun 2026) | 0.741 vs 0.718 incumbent |
| Stability | PSI against development sample, by month | Max 0.09, within 0.25 threshold |
| Segment performance | AUC by tenure band and product | Range 0.69–0.77; weakest on <6mth tenure |
| Reason-code integrity | Manual trace, 40 sampled decisions | 38 of 40 traced cleanly; 2 exceptions, see §4 |
| Override behaviour | Replay of 2025 agent overrides | No systematic reversal detected |
| Champion/challenger | Parallel run, 6 weeks, 12% traffic | Contact-to-resolution +3.1pp |

Performance testing above establishes whether the model discriminates. The tests below
establish whether it behaves acceptably under the conditions it will actually meet in
production, which is a separate question and was scoped as such.

| Test | Method | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Robustness | Feature perturbation within observed ranges, 500 replicates | Tier ranking stable in 97.4% of cases |
| Stress | Replay of Mar 2020 and Sep 2023 arrears spikes | No degradation beyond tolerance; PSI 0.14 at peak |
| Edge case | 62 hand-built cases — nil balance, zero tenure, reactivated accounts, missing income | 59 handled; 3 defaulted to lowest priority, accepted |
| Bias and fairness | Selection and outcome rates across age band, gender, residential status | Largest disparity 1.06:1, inside the 1.25 tolerance in FAIR-02 |
| Adversarial and security | Input-manipulation attempts against the scoring API; model-inversion probe on reason codes | No priority manipulation achieved; reason codes do not leak feature values |
| Integration and UAT | Two-week UAT on the pre-production platform, mirrored production data volumes, Collections Ops as testers | 11 defects raised, 11 closed, sign-off attached at Appendix D |

Full test scripts and outputs are retained in the validation workspace
(IMV/2026/041/tests) and were re-executed by the reviewer rather than accepted from the
development team's notebooks. Where results differed from those reported by Collections
Analytics in their own testing, the difference is noted in Appendix B — in both cases the
difference was immaterial and attributable to holdout construction.

## 3. Opinion

**Fit for deployment, with conditions.** The model performs adequately against its stated
purpose and the development documentation is sufficient to reconstruct the build. Two
conditions attach, both of which the Model Owner has accepted:

1. Monthly PSI monitoring on the <6mth tenure segment for the first two quarters, reported
   to this function. Not a blocker to deployment.
2. The reason-code exceptions at §4 to be resolved before the model is extended to the
   secured book.

## 4. Exceptions raised

Two of the forty sampled decisions produced a top reason code that could not be reproduced
from the feature contributions. Collections Analytics attribute this to a rounding
behaviour in the reason-code service rather than the model itself. We have not verified
that explanation and have logged it as an open item (IMV-OI-2026-19) rather than closing
it. It does not change the opinion above.
