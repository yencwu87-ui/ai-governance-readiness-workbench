## Why this page exists

Ops kept asking where the test evidence for the new collections model lives, so this is the
single page. Everything below was run on the 2026-07-02 candidate. If you need the
notebooks they're in the `crs-2026-04` repo under `/validation`, tag `v1.4.0`.

## Test results

Ran the standard battery from the analytics playbook plus two extras Priya added after the
tenure issue we hit on the last model.

- **AUC (out-of-time, Jan–Jun 26):** 0.741. Incumbent CRS-2024-11 gets 0.718 on the same
  window. Comfortable improvement, and it holds across the six months rather than being
  driven by one good month.
- **PSI vs development sample:** max 0.09 by month, well inside the 0.25 we use as the
  action threshold.
- **Segment breakdown:** AUC by tenure band and product, range 0.69 to 0.77. Weakest is
  the under-6-month tenure cohort at 0.69. Discussed at squad review 22 Jul — agreed it's
  acceptable given that cohort is 4% of contacted volume, and we'll watch it.
- **Reason codes:** traced 40 sampled decisions by hand. 38 clean. 2 where the top reason
  code didn't reconstruct from feature contributions — Wei Ming looked at it and thinks
  it's the rounding in the reason-code service, not us. Raised CA-4502 to confirm.
- **Champion/challenger:** 6 weeks parallel at 12% of traffic. Contact-to-resolution up
  3.1pp. This is the number we're taking to the steering committee.
- **Fairness screen:** ran the standard cuts. Nothing outside tolerance. Full output in
  the notebook.
