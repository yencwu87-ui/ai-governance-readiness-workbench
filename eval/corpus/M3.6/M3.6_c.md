## 1. Purpose

This standard will set out the testing and validation expected of models before first
deployment and at periodic review, so that the Group can demonstrate models perform as
intended and that weaknesses are identified by someone other than the person who built
them.

## 2. Scope

All models within the Model Inventory, including machine-learning models and AI systems
that inform customer-affecting decisions. Vendor models are in scope; the standard will
set out in Annex 2 how testing expectations apply where the Group cannot access the
underlying build. Annex 2 is not drafted.

## 3. Testing expected before deployment

Model owners will be expected to evidence, at minimum:

- discrimination and calibration on an out-of-time sample
- population stability against the development sample
- performance broken down by material customer segment
- explainability output traceable to feature contributions, where the model class permits
- a documented champion/challenger comparison where an incumbent model exists

The specific thresholds for each test will be set per materiality tier in Annex 1. Annex 1
is in drafting with Analytics and is expected in October.

## 4. Independent review

Models at tier 1 to 3 will require review by a function independent of the build team
before first deployment. Independent means no reporting line into the developing function
and no contribution to the build. The reviewer will form and issue an opinion; the model
owner may correct facts but will not have sight of the opinion before issue.

Tier 4 and 5 models will require documented peer review within the developing function.

## 5. Periodic revalidation

Annual for tier 1 and 2, biennial for tier 3, on material change otherwise. Cadence to be
confirmed against the revised Model Risk Policy.

## 6. Implementation

Once approved, this standard takes effect for models entering the deployment pipeline from
1 January 2027. Models already in production at that date will be brought into scope at
their next scheduled revalidation. A gap assessment of the existing inventory against this
standard has not yet been performed and will be commissioned after approval.

## 7. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Model Owner | Produce the testing evidence set out at §3 |
| Independent Model Validation | Perform reviews under §4 and issue opinions |
| Model Risk Committee | Approve this standard; receive validation opinions |
| Internal Audit | Periodic assurance over the operation of this standard |
