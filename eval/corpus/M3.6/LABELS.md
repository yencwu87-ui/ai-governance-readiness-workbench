## Boundary for this control

**Defined in `requirements/mas.yaml` under `controls.M3.6`, not here.** That file is what
the assessor is judged against and what a reviewer reads. Restating it here created two
definitions of `full` for one control and they diverged. Reproduced below for reading
convenience only; if the two disagree, the requirement file wins and these labels must be
re-argued.

**Requirement.** Models are evaluated against pre-defined metrics on representative held-out
data, tested for safe behaviour under the conditions they will actually meet, and challenged
by a validator who did not build them — with the outcome recorded against a named
accountable person before production go-live, at a depth proportionate to the model's
materiality.

**Model-scoped elements (10) — every applicable one must be evidenced for `full`:**

- `e1` — Evaluation results exist for the specific model version, measured against pre-defined metrics on representative held-out data
- `e2` — Testing covers behaviour under conditions the model will meet — robustness, stress, edge case, bias and fairness, adversarial and security testing, and integration or UAT in a production-like environment
- `e3` — Validation was performed by someone who did not build the model
- `e3b` — The review covers conceptual soundness, replication, and whether the developer's own evaluation and testing were adequate
- `e4` — The validator is separate from the developing function, with no reporting line into it, and the person who signs the opinion is named
- `e4b` — The validator is separate from the business owner of the use case
- `e5` — A validation outcome is recorded and dated
- `e5d` — The recorded date precedes the production go-live date, and both dates are evidenced
- `e5b` — The validation outcome is recorded against a named accountable person
- `e5c` — The validation outcome states limitations, conditions of use and residual risk accepted

Programme-scoped (3), excluded by `settings.element_scopes: [model]`:
- `e6` — The depth of validation is proportionate to the model's materiality, tiered on impact, complexity and reliance
- `e7` — Re-validation triggers are defined — material change, model or version upgrade including vendor-pushed updates, drift threshold breach, data source change, incident, and a periodic cadence by tier
- `e8` — Third-party models are validated at use-case and system level — own evaluation set, benchmarking, guardrail testing, vendor attestations, and a contractual right to change notification

Conditional, not scored until the loader handles them:
- *third_party_model* — The validator is separate from the vendor relationship manager, and the review addresses what could not be validated because the weights were not available
- *genai* — A fixed evaluation set with groundedness, hallucination and refusal measures; prompt injection, data leakage and output-boundary testing
- *agentic* — Tool-use and action-boundary testing, and a kill switch or override for high materiality models

| Rating | Condition (from the requirement file) |
|---|---|
| full | Every model-scoped element evidenced. Conditions, exceptions or open items recorded in the validator's own opinion do not prevent full — they evidence that the control operates |
| partial | Results exist but independence is absent or not evidenced (e3, e4). Reciprocal peer review inside the developing team is partial credit, not independence |
| none | A testing standard, validation plan or intent, with no evaluation or test results |

Maturity is judged separately and is not the label under test here.
