# Change Assurance Review — AI/ML production changes, Q1 2026

Technology Risk & Control Assurance
Ref: TRCA-CH-2026-03 | Issued 8 April 2026
Reviewer: L. Fernandez, Technology Risk (2LoD)
Distribution: Head of Data Science Platform, Change Manager, CIO

---

## Purpose and population

Testing of change controls over production changes affecting deployed models. Population
was all 37 changes raised against the model-serving platform between 1 January and 31
March 2026, taken from the ServiceHub change register.

Population breakdown: 28 normal, 7 emergency, 4 raised during the 12–19 March release
freeze.

## What was tested and what we found

**Approval before implementation.** All 37 changes are present in the register with a
completed record, an implementation plan and a named implementer. The register is
well maintained — no change was found that had been implemented without any record at all,
which was the finding in the 2025 review and has been closed.

Of the 7 emergency changes, 5 carried duty manager approval at the time of implementation.
For the remaining 2 (CHG-0086311, CHG-0086402) the change record was raised retrospectively
— in one case three business days after the production action, in the other five. Both were
subsequently ratified at CAB. The production action therefore preceded any recorded
authorisation.

**Change freeze.** Of the 4 changes implemented during the 12–19 March freeze, 2 carry an
MD-approved exemption. One (CHG-0086688) carries an exemption approved by a Director, not
an MD, contrary to the freeze standard which reserves exemption authority to MD and above.
One (CHG-0086701) has no exemption on file; the implementer's note in the record states
"cleared verbally with E.S." No written record of that clearance was located.

**Privileged access.** Reconciled the 37 changes to PAM checkout records. 31 reconciled
cleanly. For 4 changes the checkout was raised against a different change number than the
one implemented under. For 2 changes (both in the emergency population) the production
action was performed under a standing privileged account held by the platform support
group rather than a change-linked checkout. In one further case the PAM team granted
elevated rights to a requester who was not a named implementer on the approved change; the
grant was not challenged at the point of issue.

**Session recording.** Recordings were retained for 30 of 37 privileged sessions. For 7,
no recording exists. The platform team advise that the recording agent was not installed
on two rebuilt hosts between 4 and 26 February, which accounts for 5. The remaining 2 are
unexplained.

**Implemented vs planned.** For 10 changes compared post-implementation verification
against the change plan. 7 matched. In 3 cases the production state differed from the
approved plan — in two, additional configuration keys were set that do not appear in the
plan; in one, a planned rollback step was not executed and the record was closed as
successful regardless. None of the three carries a plan amendment or a deviation note.

**Retraining treated as change.** Confirmed. Retraining deployments are raised as changes.

## Findings

| Ref | Finding | Rating | Owner | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F-01 | Emergency changes implemented before any recorded authorisation (2 of 7) | High | Change Manager | 30 Jun 2026 |
| F-02 | Freeze exemptions absent or approved below the required authority (2 of 4) | High | Head of Retail Technology | 30 Jun 2026 |
| F-03 | Privileged access not reconcilable to the approved change (7 instances) | High | PAM Service Owner | 31 Jul 2026 |
| F-04 | Privileged session recordings missing (7 of 37) | Medium | Platform Engineering | 31 May 2026 |
| F-05 | Production state differs from approved plan without amendment (3 of 10) | Medium | Head of Data Science Platform | 31 Jul 2026 |

## Conclusion

A change management process is in place and operating over AI/ML production changes, and
change records are consistently raised and retained. However, the controls that make those
records meaningful — authorisation ahead of implementation, freeze governance, privileged
access tied to the approved change, and verification that what was implemented matches
what was approved — did not operate consistently across the quarter. Management action is
required on F-01 to F-03 before the next quarterly review.
